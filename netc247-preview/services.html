Services

Everything your network needs, under one roof

Four service areas, one team. Explore what each one covers below.

01

Managed IT

Network security and stability starts at the desktop. A chain is only as strong as its weakest link — and that applies just as much to your IT infrastructure.

Security policies, firewalls, and antivirus/spyware protection all require steady maintenance to stay effective. We handle that maintenance so peak performance and security aren't things you have to think about.

  • 24/7 monitoring & notification
  • Remote & onsite support
  • System health & incident reporting
  • Consultations & implementation
  • Virus & spyware removal
  • System repair & upgrades
  • Data backup
  • Total IT responsibility

02

Network Services

Hardly any business can operate without a computer, and even fewer don't stand to gain from a properly designed network. Your options shouldn't be limited by an inflexible IT infrastructure.

Cloud file servers, email, VoIP, CRM, and remote access to office resources are all within reach for a business of any size — we make them a reality for yours.

  • Network design & installation
  • Wired & wireless solutions
  • Network repair
  • Office relocation
  • Mobile workforce support
  • Network security

03

Cloud Solutions

Cloud computing means delivering services and resources over the internet instead of hosting them yourself. It's not a new idea — just a better name for something businesses have relied on for years.

It's no longer expensive or complicated to bring cloud services into a small business. We help you find the right mix of public, private, or hybrid cloud for how your team actually works.

  • Email & collaboration
  • Web-based CRM
  • Web-based collaboration tools
  • Offsite backup & remote file servers
  • Public cloud
  • Private & hybrid cloud

04

Security & Low Voltage

A well-designed communications infrastructure is the difference between productive profitability and costly downtime. Your voice and data cabling is the core of how your business communicates.

We design and install cabling infrastructure with careful planning for both your current needs and future growth — plus the surveillance and access control systems to keep your space secure.

  • Cabling design & installation
  • Data, voice & video cabling
  • Tone, test, label & qualify existing infrastructure
  • Video distribution
  • Security surveillance
  • Access control

Tell us what's going on with your systems and we'll point you in the right direction.
