About Us

20+ years of keeping Central Florida businesses online

We're a small team that believes every small business deserves an efficient, productive IT infrastructure — regardless of budget or size.

networks | etc provides onsite computer and network consultation, service, and installation across the Orlando area. We work with clients of all sizes, from single-office small businesses to larger organizations with more complex needs.

We're a collection of industry professionals dedicated to providing high-tech solutions at a reasonable rate. Technology changes constantly — deciding what equipment to use, how to manage and back up your data, and when to replace aging systems can be overwhelming on your own. That's what we're here for.

Our team has over 20 years of combined experience across a wide range of small business and enterprise technologies. We specialize in finding the right custom solution for your organization, not a one-size-fits-all package.

Working with networks | etc means having a team of skilled IT professionals on hand and on call, without the overhead of hiring one in-house.

Our Mission

Technology should simplify your business, not complicate it

We continually educate ourselves, our clients, and our community on existing and emerging technologies, and we look for creative ways to put those technologies to work. Whether the tools are open source, proprietary, or a mix of both, we stay genuinely curious about what's coming next — because that curiosity is what lets us do our jobs better, and do right by the businesses that depend on us.
