Ordering, payment, and shipping questions

Everything you need to know before placing your first order.

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

You can phone us, fax us, or email your request and we will price it and call you with the details. New customers should open this new customer information form, print it, fill in the requested information, and fax or mail it to us.

How do I pay for my first order?

For the first order we require credit card or bank wire prepayment. There is a fee for wire transfers.

How do I establish payment terms with you?

Open this credit application, print and fill it out, and fax, mail, or email it to us. We will respond with an answer within 30 days.

What if I need plants that are not listed on your availability sheets?

Not a problem. We are known for our ability to locate hard to find product. Call us with your specs and we will get to work.

Can you ship to Canada?

Yes, we just need a few days notice to prepare the phytosanitary certificates.

How do you package plants for shipping?

You may request single sleeve, double sleeved, poly wrap, or boxed packaging.

Can you bar code the plants I order?

For a nominal fee, we can print your supplied bar code and place bar code stickers on the pots.

What if I don't have a trucking firm to ship from Florida?

We can take care of that. With our contacts in the shipping industry, we will make sure your plants are picked up by a trucking company that delivers in your area.

Still have a question? Get in touch and we will help you sort it out.
