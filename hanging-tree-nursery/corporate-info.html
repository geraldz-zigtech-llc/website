Established 1981

Hanging Tree Nursery, Inc. is a wholesale foliage broker specializing in florist grade indoor tropical foliage ranging from 3 inch to 30 inch pot size. We contract to buy only the best quality sun and shade grown material from growers throughout the state of Florida, and we are fully dedicated to making it easy for our customers to fill their plant needs at a true one stop source for foliage and tropical plants.

Since 1981 we have grown from a small backyard nursery to nearly 10 acres of double poly greenhouses, shade houses, loading docks, and packing shed, where we source and consolidate some of the finest spathiphyllum available. Our packing shed and loading area feature easy dock access, and we ship by freight service as well as our own trucks.
