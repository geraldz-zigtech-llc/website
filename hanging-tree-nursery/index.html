Corporate Info

A true one stop source, since 1981

Hanging Tree Nursery, Inc. is a wholesale foliage broker specializing in florist grade indoor tropical foliage ranging from 3 inch to 30 inch pot size. We contract with the best sun and shade grown material from growers across Florida, and we source and consolidate some of the finest spathiphyllum available.

We ship by freight service as well as our own trucks, and our staff bring over 225 years of combined experience in the foliage industry to every order.
