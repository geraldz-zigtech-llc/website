Concept demo

Switchable smart glass — clear to opaque, on command

Three ways to build the same effect for a glass & window film site: an instant toggle, an animated crossfade, and a draggable before/after reveal. Same illustrated panel, three interaction feels — pick the one that fits the brand.

1. Click to toggle — instant swap

No animation. The glass state changes the instant the button is clicked — feels mechanical and immediate, like flipping a light switch.

Interaction A

Instant switch

Best for showing raw functionality quickly — closest to how the actual PDLC glass behaves in real life (milliseconds).

Click the glass panel to toggle. Keyboard: Tab to it, then Enter or Space.

2. Click with animated crossfade

Same toggle interaction, but the frost dissolves in and out over about half a second — reads as more premium and deliberate on a marketing site.

Interaction B

Animated dissolve

Slightly slower, but visually satisfying — the transition itself becomes part of the brand moment rather than just a state change.

Click the glass panel to toggle. Keyboard: Tab to it, then Enter or Space.

3. Draggable before/after slider

Drag (or use arrow keys, fully keyboard accessible) to reveal how much of the panel is clear vs. frosted — good for a hero section where you want visitors to physically interact rather than just click.

ClearOpaque
Interaction C

Before / after reveal

Familiar pattern from home-renovation sites, adapted here. Works well as a hands-on hero element rather than a simple button click.
