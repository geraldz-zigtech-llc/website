Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide directly: your name, email address, phone number, and message. We do not collect this information through any other means on this site.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your inquiry and to provide the services you request. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Contact Form Processing

Our contact form is processed by a third-party form delivery service, which transmits your submission to our email inbox. We do not store submissions in a separate database.

Analytics

This site may use website analytics to understand how visitors use our pages, in aggregate and anonymized form, to help us improve the site. Analytics data is not linked to your name or contact information unless you separately submit our contact form.

Cookies

This site may use minimal cookies required for basic site functionality and anonymized analytics. We do not use cookies for cross-site advertising tracking.

Your Rights

You may request that we delete any information you've submitted to us by contacting us at info@aspireonnetworks.com.
