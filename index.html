FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a HUBZone-certified IT contractor?

A HUBZone-certified IT contractor is a small business that the Small Business Administration has verified as being located in and employing residents of a Historically Underutilized Business Zone. HUBZone certification gives contracting officers a 10 percent price evaluation preference in full and open competitions and allows agencies to set aside contracts exclusively for HUBZone firms. ZigTech, LLC is SBA-certified HUBZone, based in Cocoa, Florida.

Who does SDVOSB IT contracting near Cocoa, Florida?

ZigTech, LLC is a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business based in Cocoa, Florida, providing network observability, cybersecurity, and IT infrastructure services to federal agencies and commercial clients. ZigTech is SBA-certified SDVOSB and eligible for sole-source and set-aside awards, including VA contracts.

What is network observability?

Network observability is the practice of using real-time telemetry, anomaly detection, and analytics to understand the health and performance of a network, rather than relying solely on traditional uptime monitoring. It gives IT teams visibility into multi-vendor, on-premises, cloud, and hybrid environments so problems can be identified before they cause outages. ZigTech specializes in network observability and AIOps for both federal and commercial networks.

Does ZigTech only work with federal agencies, or also state, local, education, and commercial clients?

ZigTech works across all four. On the federal side, ZigTech supports civilian and defense agencies, VA and DoD programs, and prime contractors through its SDVOSB, VOSB, and HUBZone certifications. ZigTech also serves SLED clients, including state agencies, county and municipal government, and school districts and universities. On the commercial (B2B) side, ZigTech provides network engineering, managed IT services, and cybersecurity support to mid-market enterprises, healthcare networks, financial services firms, and small businesses modernizing their web presence.
