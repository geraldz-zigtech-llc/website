Managed IT services intake

Let's scope your IT environment

A managed IT services assessment is a short intake process that helps us understand your current environment: how many users and endpoints you support, what backup and security measures are already in place, and where your current setup is falling short. Whether you have an informal internal setup or an existing provider you are not happy with, this information lets us walk into a discovery call with real context instead of starting from zero. It typically takes ten to fifteen minutes and is the first step toward a right sized managed IT proposal.
